Core CampusLoop Capabilities

Everything you need to exchange knowledge seamlessly, build meaningful campus connections, and learn without paying money.

1. College Selection

Choose your country, city, and college/university to anchor yourself in your local campus community and connect with peers who share your academic calendar.

2. Verified Student Profiles

Profiles spotlight student name, verified college, department, academic year (freshman to senior), bio, skills to teach, and skills they want to learn.

3. Skill Upload

List the skills you can teach: coding, graphic design, video editing, communication, languages, or engineering, complete with learning topics and availability.

4. Campus-Based Discovery

Find seniors who aced the courses you are taking, juniors seeking guidance, and classmates from your department with 1-click campus filters.

5. Cross-College Learning

Expand beyond your borders. Learn from peers at Stanford, Oxford, IIT Madras, NUS, TU Munich, and nearby colleges through virtual 1-on-1 sessions.

6. Skill Search & Requests

Fast search with multi-filter keywords. Send direct structured learning requests specifying your learning goals, proposed time slot, and exchange offer.

Zero Fees Guaranteed

7. Free Skill Exchange: Knowledge Barter For Everyone

No subscriptions, no hidden tokens, and no payment walls. You teach what you know, and learn what you need. A pure, egalitarian community for university students worldwide.
